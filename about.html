About MediaHunt

MediaHunt is a fast, dark-themed media discovery and creator archive directory engineered with pure HTML5, CSS3, and modular Vanilla JavaScript.

Platform Architecture

  • Zero Mandatory Backend: Powered by a centralized assets/js/data.js catalog and client-side URL query routing, enabling instant deployment on GitHub Pages or any static CDN.
  • Persistent Local Bookmarks: Your saved favorites are stored directly in your browser via localStorage without requiring account registration.
  • Responsive Multi-Page Structure: Dedicated views for category browsing, trending leaderboards, real-time keyword/tag search, and HTML5 video playback with archive inspection.
