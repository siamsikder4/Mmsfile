Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Client-Side Storage Only

MediaHunt operates as a static client-side web application. We do not collect, transmit, or store personal accounts on external servers. Features such as your Saved Library rely exclusively on your browser's localStorage under the key mediahunt_favorites_v1.

2. External Media Streams

Video previews streamed on individual detail pages load directly from licensed public media CDNs over HTTPS. Standard network request headers (such as IP address and browser user-agent) are processed by those CDN providers to deliver video playback.

3. Clearing Your Data

You can clear all locally saved bookmarks at any time from the Saved Library page or through your browser's site data settings.
